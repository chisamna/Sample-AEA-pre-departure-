Before you go

  • Be sure to wear comfortable shoes and be prepared for lots of walking.
  • Bring a comfortable backpack as your personal item and for daily use as needed.
  • Bring a reusable water bottle and stay hydrated.
  • Pack light!
  • In Europe, rooms and spacing between beds can be smaller than what we are used to in the U.S.
  • Participants with food allergies and dietary concerns — please confirm all dietary requirements directly with the staff at your accommodations and food venues.
  • Please also ensure that anyone with a severe allergy is equipped with an epi-pen if an adrenaline injection must be administered, and ensure a nominated person from the group is available to assist.
  • Tips have already been included for your coach drivers, assistants, and guides.
  • Tips for meals are at your own discretion.
  • Hostel is booked under: College of Charleston — Booking Ref: J5-31S.

Accommodation

Three Little Pigs Hostel

Stresemannstraße 66, 10963 Berlin, Germany  ·  +49 30 26395880  ·  three-little-pigs.de

Three Little Pigs Hostel is situated in a former convent, 10 minutes by foot from Checkpoint Charlie and Potsdamer Platz Square. Within a short walk is the S Anhalter Bahnhof underground station with easy connections throughout the city. The accommodation features a 24-hour front desk and free Wi-Fi throughout the property. Rooms do not have A/C. The neighborhood of Kreuzberg is known for its diverse cultural life and is a hub for local artists.
